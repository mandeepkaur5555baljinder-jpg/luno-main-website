Luno — a personal AI that feels like yours

Luno is a chat, voice, vision and code assistant, built end to end by one person. Open it in your browser today, or add it to your iPhone home screen.

Try Luno at app.lunoai.in

Five ways in

  • Chat: conversations that hold together.
  • Voice: full back-and-forth speech, interruptions included.
  • Vision: reads whiteboards, screenshots and handwritten notes.
  • Code: writes and explains real code.
  • Reasoning: works hard problems through step by step.

Private to your account

Your preferences and past conversations carry over between sessions and stay private to your own account.
